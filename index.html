MEET MARIA. MEET THE FAMILY.

FAMILY AT
THE HEART
OF IT ALL.

Maria brings more than two decades of life in the United States and four years of running a store in Massachusetts to Guallpa Latin Market. Looking for new opportunities, she moved to Vermont and opened her market in South Burlington.

This is a family-owned grocery store, built around the people and traditions that matter most to Maria. She runs the market day to day, with her family by her side. Come discover Latin foods and a unique selection of everyday finds.
